**pyBuildingEnergy 2.3.0** extends the cooling system modules (EN 16798-9, EN 16798-13, EN 16798-15) and adds a coupled cooling chain.

`pip install --upgrade pybuildingenergy==2.3.0`

- **Coupled chain** (`CoolingChainCalculator`): distribution, storage and generation are solved together. The storage and generator agree on the available energy at each hour, a shortfall in charging is kept apart from a shortfall for the users, and the actual generator and storage outlet temperatures are fed back to the distribution, formulas (23) and (24) of EN 16798-9. An optional terminal capacity per circuit (library extension) separates a temperature shortfall from an energy shortfall.
- **EN 16798-9**: several circuits with priorities and allocation of the unmet energy (`distribute_shortfall`); `MAX_TMP` now uses the circuit and AHU requirements.
- **EN 16798-13**: absorption chillers (9d), (21)–(23); OTHER generators (9g), (24); several generators with priorities (7)–(12); heat-rejection operation 6.4.2.4 (13)–(14) with TEMP and MAX_POWER control; capacity correction (9a)–(9c); part-load method of 6.3.2.2.2.1 with C1–C4 and Δθcorr fitted from EN 14825 points; indices per energy carrier (electricity, absorption heat).
- **EN 16798-15**: state of charge, control strategies (6a)–(6c), stratified tanks, ice and PCM storage charged only down to temperatures the generator can reach.
- **Fixes**: HYBRID free cooling compared with the wet bulb; `minimum_part_load_ratio` is now used; backup electricity is included in the rejected heat. These can change cooling results for configurations that used those options.
- Each item is labelled in `docs/cooling_16798_audit.html` as a formula from the standard, an assumption or a library extension. Reference values from the standards are not distributed.
