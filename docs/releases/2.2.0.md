**pyBuildingEnergy 2.2.0**: EN 16798-5-1 air handling unit extensions, and new EN 16798-5-2 and EN 16798-7 modules.

`pip install --upgrade pybuildingenergy==2.2.0`

Default AHU configurations give the same results as 2.1.7 (checked on 54,000 hourly steps).

- EN 16798-5-1 AHU: moisture processes (humidification, cooling/dehumidification),
  heat recovery with Annex D (EPB Center spreadsheet version by default, 2017 print with
  `annex_d_version: "2017"`), duct/AHU leakage, recirculation, frost protection, fans and
  auxiliary energy, ground preheating (Annex C), unbalanced flows, supply temperature
  control, central multizone AHU with VAV (`central_ahus`), national data sets `EN` and `IT`.
- EN 16798-5-2 (`ventilation_16798_5_2`): monthly/yearly/bin method including the extract air
  heat pump. Standalone, not called by the simulation engine.
- EN 16798-7 (`ventilation_16798_7`): zone air flow paths and mass balance (method 1),
  coupled to the zone ventilation through an `en16798_7` component (one per zone).
- Inconsistent or non-physical inputs raise `ValueError` (unsolvable mass balance, NaN,
  out-of-range values, efficiency outside [0, 1]).
- Known deviations from the printed standards (documented in CHANGELOG.rst): sign of the
  extract duct loss, fan heat counted once, (47)/(48) of EN 16798-7 read from a truncated scan,
  and the double domestic hot water deduction in (46) of EN 16798-5-2, kept as printed
  (`HeatPumpData(deduct_dhw_energy_46=False)` removes it).
