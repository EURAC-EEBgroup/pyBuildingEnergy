EN 16798 Cooling System Implementation Audit

Audit map for the cooling-side modules added around the heat-pump example: EN 16798-9 operating conditions, EN 16798-15 cooling storage and EN 16798-13 cooling generation.

Scope And Decision

EN 15316-2 And EN 15316-3

Cooling emission and water-based cooling distribution were already present in EmissionSystemCalculator and DistributionSystemCalculator. The example chain now explicitly feeds the cooling-side outputs of those modules into the EN 16798 cooling modules.

EN 16798 Integration

The attached standards are relevant because EN 15316-4-2 is a heating and DHW heat-pump generation method. Space cooling is now handled by EN 16798-9, EN 16798-15 and EN 16798-13 by default, while the earlier reversible heat-pump cooling calculation remains available as a simplified comparison path.

Implemented Cooling Boundary

The default examples model a water-based room cooling system, chilled-water buffer storage and an air-to-water compression cooling generator using EER/capacity product-map data. The modules also cover several circuits with priorities, chilled-water (mixed or stratified), ice and PCM storage with a state of charge, compression, absorption and OTHER generators with priorities, free cooling and the dry / wet / hybrid heat rejection of EN 16798-13 6.4.2.4.

Default Example Chain

The full path is the default. It keeps the EN 15316 modules for room emission, water distribution and heating/DHW storage, then uses EN 16798 for cooling operating conditions, cooling storage and cooling generation. 

ISO 52016 sensible heating/cooling needs
  -> EN 15316-2 emission effects for heating and cooling
  -> EN 12831-3 DHW energy-need profile
  -> EN 16798-9 cooling operating conditions
  -> EN 15316-3 heating/cooling/DHW distribution
  -> EN 15316-5 heating/DHW storage
  -> EN 16798-15 cooling storage
  -> EN 14511/EN 14825 product performance data
  -> EN 16798-13 cooling generation
  -> EN 15316-4-2 heating/DHW heat-pump generation

Coupled storage - generator balance

CoolingChainCalculator closes the loop that a purely sequential chain leaves open: the storage request is checked against what the generator can supply at the temperature the storage requires, and the storage is simulated again with that limit until both agree. Each stage receives one explicit demand column. 

EN 16798-9   Q_C_gen_in_req, required flow temperatures (circuits)
  -> EN 16798-15  state of charge, request to the generator, formula (1) temperature
  -> EN 16798-13  availability at that temperature (free cooling + generators + backup)
  -> EN 16798-15  again with Q_C_sto_gen_avl_kWh: users before charging,
                  emergency discharge, charge really received
  ... until the storage request <= availability (usually 2 iterations)
  -> EN 16798-9   again with the actual generator / storage outlet temperatures:
                  effective flow (23) and return (24) temperatures
  -> terminals    (optional) cooling deliverable at that temperature; the circuit
                  demand is limited to it and the chain re-run (outer iteration)
  -> EN 16798-9   distribute_shortfall: lowest-priority circuits lose first,
                  equal priorities and zones of a circuit pro rata

The shortfall is split into a temperature part (Q_C_dis_unmet_temp_kWh_<circuit>: water too warm for the terminals) and an energy part (Q_C_dis_unmet_gen_kWh_<circuit>: storage / generator short). h_C_dis_flw_not_reached counts the hours with demand in which the required flow temperature is not reached. Without terminal data a warm supply is reported but not converted into a shortfall. Always check converged: a RuntimeWarning is raised when an iteration stops at max_iterations.

Outputs: Q_C_sto_unmet_kWh (shortfall towards the users), Q_C_sto_chg_unmet_kWh (charge the store wanted but did not receive), Q_C_dis_unmet_kWh_<circuit>, Q_C_del_kWh_<zone>, iterations and convergence. Both examples use the coupled chain when EN 16798-15 and EN 16798-13 are selected.

Status labels used below: implemented formula of the standard; assumption choice where the standard is silent or ambiguous; extension library feature beyond the standard; fallback default data.

In the default path, cooling electricity is reported by CoolingGenerationSystemCalculator. Heating and DHW electricity are still reported by HeatPumpSystemCalculator. The example writes combined_generation_summary.csv so the inspection plots and annual cards show one combined generator view.

Main Calculation Formulas

EN 16798-9 Cooling Operating Conditions

Code: CoolingSystemCalculator._distribution_flow_temperature()

CONST: theta_C_dis_in_flw_req = theta_C_dis_flw_set
ODA_COMP: theta_C_dis_in_flw_req = clip(f_e * theta_e + delta_theta_off, theta_min, theta_max)

Code: CoolingSystemCalculator._volume_flow() and _return_temperature()

variable flow: q_V_C_dis = q_V_C_dis_des * Phi_C / Phi_C_des
fallback flow: q_V_C_dis = Q_C / (1.16 * DeltaT_des * t)
theta_C_dis_out_ret_req = theta_C_dis_in_flw_req + Q_C / (1.16 * q_V_C_dis * t)

Code: CoolingSystemCalculator._simulate()

Q_C_gen_in_req = Q_C_dis_out_tot_req + Q_C_dis_ls_simplified + f_wat_C_aux_dis * W_C_aux_dis_simplified

EN 16798-15 Cooling Storage

Code: CoolingStorageSystemCalculator._simulate()

Q_C_sto_out_ls = H_C_sto_out_ls * max(theta_amb - theta_C_gen_out, 0) * t / 1000
Q_C_sto_in_ls = H_C_sto_in_ls * max(theta_amb - theta_C_sto_out, 0) * t / 1000
Q_C_sto_ls = H_C_sto_tot_ls * max(theta_amb - theta_C_sto, 0) * t / 1000
Q_C_sto_ls_tot = Q_C_sto_out_ls + Q_C_sto_in_ls + Q_C_sto_ls

Code: CoolingStorageSystemCalculator._pump_runtime()

t_pump = min(Q_C / (1.16 * q_V_pump * DeltaT_pump), t_step)
W_C_sto_aux = t_pump_in * P_pump_in + t_pump_out * P_pump_out

Code: CoolingStorageSystemCalculator._state_of_charge() (each step)

Q_C_sto_dis = min(Q_C_sto_stored, f_C_sto_ctrl * Q_C_sto_out) (5); generator short: max(., Q_C_sto_out - Q_avl)
Q_C_sto_direct = min(Q_C_sto_out - Q_C_sto_dis, Q_avl); Q_C_sto_unmet = Q_C_sto_out - Q_C_sto_dis - Q_C_sto_direct
Q_C_sto_chg = min(wanted charge, Q_avl - Q_C_sto_direct); Q_C_sto_chg_unmet = wanted - Q_C_sto_chg
Q_avl = (Q_C_sto_gen_avl - fixed pump heat) / (1 + pump heat per kWh) - Q_C_sto_out_ls - Q_C_sto_in_ls
Q_C_sto_in = Q_C_sto_direct + Q_C_sto_chg + Q_C_sto_out_ls + Q_C_sto_in_ls + W_C_sto_aux_to_medium

EN 16798-13 Cooling Generation

Code: CoolingGenerationSystemCalculator._simulate() and _capacity_and_eer()

theta_C_gen_out = max(theta_C_gen_out_req, theta_C_gen_out_limit)
Q_C_gen_in_max = capacity_kW(theta_cond_in, theta_C_gen_out) * t * f_op_C
Q_C_gen_in = Q_C_free + Q_C_mechanical + Q_C_backup
f_C_PL = Q_C_mechanical / Q_C_gen_in_max
E_C_gen_el_in = Q_C_mechanical / EER(theta_cond_in, theta_C_gen_out)
EN 14825 active: EER_C_gen = EER_C_gen_full_load * CR / (Cd * CR + (1 - Cd))
Q_C_gen_out = Q_C_gen_in + E_C_gen_el_in
E_C_total = E_C_gen_el_in + E_C_backup_in + W_C_aux_gen
SEER_C_gen = Q_C_gen_in / E_C_total
ABS: zeta_c = (T0 + theta_evap)(theta_H - theta_cond) / ((T0 + theta_H)(theta_cond - theta_evap)) (23)
ABS: Q_max = Phi_n * t * p(zeta_c) / p(zeta_c,n) (9d); Q_H_C_gen_abs_in = Q_C / zeta (21)
OTHER: Q_max = Phi_n * t * f(theta_C_gen_out_req, theta_sk) (9g); E_el = Q_C * f(theta_C_gen_out, theta_sk, f_C_PL) (24)
HYBRID: f_op_hr_wet = 1 if theta_e > theta_lim_wet_hr (TEMP) or Q_req > sum Q_max_dry (MAX_POWER) (13d)-(13g)
W_hr = Q_hr * ((1 - f_op_hr_wet) p_hr_el_dry + f_op_hr_wet p_hr_el_wet) (26b)

Known Limits And Audit Notes

  • EN 16798-9 is implemented as the connector for water-based cooling operating temperatures and flow; detailed room emission remains in EN 15316-2 and pipe/pump distribution remains in EN 15316-3.
  • EN 16798-15: chilled-water (mixed or stratified), ice and PCM storage with a state of charge. Not modelled: PCM hysteresis and supercooling, the heat-transfer fluid inside latent stores, heat release to an ambient colder than the medium.
  • EN 16798-13: compression, absorption and OTHER generators, several generators with priorities and the heat-rejection operation of 6.4.2.4. Adsorption, desiccant and evaporative cooling are only reachable as OTHER generators with user functions. The EN 14825 part-load correction is defined for compression chillers; for absorption chillers it is a library extension.
  • Indices: SEER_C_gen is cooling over electricity only. For absorption chillers the driving heat is a separate carrier: use zeta_C_gen_seasonal and EER_C_gen_all_inputs (cooling over electricity plus driving heat), or weight the carriers in the EN ISO 52000-1 accounting.
  • The distribution shortfall assumes the network losses and auxiliary heat are supplied; with a strongly undersized supply this overstates what reaches the users. Use either the simplified EN 16798-9 losses or the detailed EN 15316-3 losses, not both.
  • The terminal model is a single characteristic per circuit (mean water temperature, exponent n); it does not model latent cooling, valve authority or flow limits.
  • The coupled chain assumes that the generator availability does not depend on the load (it is evaluated for an unlimited request at the temperature required by the storage) and that the generator reaches max(theta_C_sto_C, theta_C_gen_out_limit_C) when charging.
  • Reference values, ranges and tables of the standards are not reproduced in the repository; the tests check formulae and independent energy balances.
  • Example defaults are realistic demonstration assumptions for 120 m2 residential cases. Project-grade studies should replace them with project geometry, pump data, storage product data, national annex values and manufacturer performance maps.
  • The older reversible heat-pump cooling branch remains available through --calculation-path heat-pump-cooling or --cooling-generation-method heat-pump-simple for comparison with previous outputs.
The standards use module boundaries and sign conventions that can look surprising in a building-energy time series. In these cooling modules, positive Q_C means useful cooling removed from the space or system, while recoverable heat gains on the cooling side are reported with negative sign where the standard applies that convention.
