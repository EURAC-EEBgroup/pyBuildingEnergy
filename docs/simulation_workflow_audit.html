European EPB System Simulation Workflow Audit

This page is the parent audit trail for the Athens and Bolzano European EPB examples. It shows how the implemented standards connect to one another, which files carry data between modules, and where to continue the detailed clause-by-clause review.

Default Full Workflow

Example building geometry and schedules
  -> EN ISO 52016-1 sensible heating/cooling needs
  -> EN 15316-2 room emission and control effects for heating/cooling
  -> EN 12831-3 DHW energy-need profile and DHW design sizing
  -> EN 16798-9 cooling operating conditions and chilled-water handoff
  -> EN 15316-3 heating/cooling/DHW water distribution losses and pump auxiliaries
  -> EN 15316-5 heating/DHW storage losses and storage auxiliaries
  -> EN 16798-15 cooling storage heat gains and storage auxiliaries
  -> EN 14511 / EN 14825 product capacity, COP/EER and part-load performance data
  -> optional EN 15316-4-3 / EN 15316-4-6 solar thermal and PV
  -> EN 15316-4-2 heating/DHW heat-pump generation
     or EN 15316-4-1 boiler, EN 15316-4-4 CHP, EN 15316-4-5 district heat
  -> EN 16798-13 cooling generation
  -> combined generator summaries, CSV outputs and HTML inspection plots
Default calculation path: python examples/heat_pump_15316_4_2_example.py --scenario athens --calculation-path full. Replace athens with bolzano for the Italian example. Use --calculation-path full-renewables, --calculation-path boiler-full, --calculation-path chp-full or --calculation-path district-full to exercise the newly added generation branches.

--calculation-path boiler-fullRuns the full upstream/cooling chain and switches heating/DHW generation to EN 15316-4-1.
--calculation-path chp-fullRuns the full upstream/cooling chain and switches heating/DHW generation to EN 15316-4-4.
--calculation-path district-fullRuns the full upstream/cooling chain and switches heating/DHW generation to EN 15316-4-5.

Known Workflow Boundaries

  • EN 12831-3 is implemented for DHW energy needs and DHW system design sizing with storage switch points, time lag, effective reheating power and supply curves.
  • Project-specific direct-flow fixture sizing, declared storage standby losses and special tapping programs can be supplied from external references such as EN 806-3, EN 12897/product data or EN 13203-2 when those inputs are required.
  • EN 14511 / EN 14825 support product rating maps and part-load correction; the complete regulatory SCOP/SEER labelling procedure is not implemented.
  • EN 16798-13 is implemented for compression cooling generation in the examples; non-compression generators are not implemented.
  • The default cooling generation is EN 16798-13. Heating and DHW generation default to EN 15316-4-2 heat pumps, with EN 15316-4-1, EN 15316-4-4 and EN 15316-4-5 alternatives available by CLI switch.
  • Solar thermal and PV are implemented as a deterministic time-series branch. Formal compliance studies should replace example yields with project, product or national-annex data.
